What is drawn by rule

  • The room either side of a cell's text is the cell font's own digit, not a constant: a 6–8 pixel digit gets 3 before and 2 after, a 17-pixel one 6 and 5.
  • One level of indent is three spaces of the workbook's first font — 15 pixels in ＭＳ Ｐゴシック 11, 12 in 游ゴシック 11, 24 in ＭＳ ゴシック 11.
  • Every ground goes down before any of the words, so a letter hanging over its neighbour is not wiped by the next cell's fill.
  • A row's height is worked out, not read: the stored number is a cache unless the row pins it.
  • A line breaks where the characters run out of room, one character's advance at a time, and never before a mark that may not start a line.

What this page is not

The browser measures the text, so a line that lands within a pixel of an edge may break here and not in Excel. The pixel-exact path is the native renderer in tools/oxi-xlsx-renderer, which is what the 285-workbook comparison is run against.
